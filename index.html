🔍 What is Open-Jev?

Open-Jev is a revolutionary decision-making tool that works differently from traditional AI models. Instead of generating long text answers that need to be parsed and interpreted, Open-Jev directly provides typed probabilities for your chosen candidates.

Imagine asking your app: "Which action should I take?" and getting immediate, precise probabilities like 87% for Option A, 10% for Option B, and 3% for Option C. No fuss, no ambiguity—just clear numerical guidance to make better decisions.

🛠️ How Open-Jev Works

  1. Provide Context: Share the situation or background information that the decision depends on.
  2. Ask Questions: Specify what you need to decide. For example: "Which marketing strategy should we use?"
  3. List Candidates: Provide the options to evaluate. These are your potential choices.
  4. Get Probabilities: Open-Jev immediately returns a percentage for each candidate, showing the likelihood of each being the right choice.

💡 Pro Tip: The clearer your context and questions, the more accurate and useful your probabilities will be.
